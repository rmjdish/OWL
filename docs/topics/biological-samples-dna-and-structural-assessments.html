Biological Samples, DNA and Structural Assessments

Biological samples were first collected at age 53, and expanded considerably at the age 60-64 clinic visit to include blood, urine and saliva samples alongside cardiac, vascular, bone and body composition assessments. DNA extracted from blood and cheek swabs supports an extensive genetic research programme, including genome-wide association and epigenomic studies.
