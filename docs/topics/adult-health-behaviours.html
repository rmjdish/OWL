Adult Health Behaviours

The NSHD has gathered a wide range of information on people's health behaviours — the actions, habits and lifestyle choices that can affect health for good or bad — including detailed histories of smoking, drinking, diet and physical activity collected repeatedly across adulthood.

Sub-topics

Sub-topicDescription
SmokingCigarette, cigar and pipe smoking habits, quit history and lifetime smoking trajectory
Drinking (alcohol)Self-reported alcohol consumption, drinking diaries and screening for hazardous drinking
Diet and nutritionDietary intake recorded through diet diaries and food frequency questionnaires across adulthood
Physical activitySelf-reported leisure-time, sport and work-related physical activity levels
