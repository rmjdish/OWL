Women: stress and urge incontinence

Each year between 1994 and 2000, women who replied positively to "Do you ever lose any urine when you cough, sneeze, laugh, run, or exercise?" were classified as having symptoms of stress incontinence. Those who replied positively to both "Do you ever have an urgent and strong desire to pass urine which is difficult to control?" and "Do you ever lose any urine before you reach the toilet?" were classified as having symptoms of urgency and urge incontinence. Severe incontinence was defined as involuntary loss occurring twice a month or more over the previous year and loss of more than a few drops of urine.

Men: bladder problems

In 1999 (age 53 years), men were asked a series of questions related to bladder problems and incontinence.

ICIQ measure (2014–16, 2022–23)

In the 2014–16 and 2022–23 postal questionnaires, all study members were asked how often, how much and when they leaked urine, and how much this interfered with everyday life. This was based on the ICIQ-urinary incontinence questionnaire.
