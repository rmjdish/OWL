MRC respiratory questionnaire

In 1966, 1971, 1977, 1982, 1989, 1999, 2006–10, 2014–16 and 2022–23, study members were asked the MRC Questionnaire on Respiratory Symptoms (MRCQ). The MRCQ consists of questions on coughing, phlegm, breathlessness, wheezing and chest illnesses.

Lung function measurements

Lung function was first measured in 1982 (age 36) using a Mini Wright Peak Flow meter to measure peak expiratory flow rate (PEFR). PEFR was also measured at 1989 and 1999 using the Micro Medical spirometer.

At ages 43 (1989), 53 (1999) and 60–64 (2006–10), forced expiratory volume in one second (FEV1) and forced vital capacity (FVC) were measured using the Micro Medical spirometer. At ages 68–70 (2014–16) and 76–77 (2022–23) the Easy on-PC sensor spirometer was used.

Recommended measures of FEV1 and FVC were derived using American Thoracic Society (ATS) criteria, taking the maximum of qualifying measures after removing those where technique was unsatisfactory. Data on air pollution were also collected as part of the 60–64 (2006–10) sweep.
