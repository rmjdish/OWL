MRC respiratory questionnaire

In 1966, 1971, 1977, 1982, 1989, 1999, 2006-10,2014-2016 and 2022-2023, study members were asked the MRC Questionnaire on Respiratory Symptoms (MRCQ).

The MRCQ consists of questions on coughing, phlegm, breathlessness, wheezing and chest illnesses.

Lung function measurements

Lung function was first measured in 1982 (age 36) using a Mini Wright Peak Flow meter to measure peak expiratory flow rate (PEFR). PEFR was also measured at 1989 and 1999 using the Micro Medical spirometer.

At ages 43 (1989), 53 (1999) and 60–64 (2006-10), forced expiratory volume in one second (FEV1) and forced vital capacity (FVC) were measured using the Micro Medical spirometer. At ages 68–70 (2014-2016) and 76–77 (2022-2023) forced expiratory volume in one second (FEV1) and forced vital capacity (FVC) was measured using Easy on-PC sensor (spirometer). Three measures were used at 43, 60-64, 68-70 and 76-77 years and two measures at 53 years. It was also recorded whether the technique for each blow was satisfactory..

Recommended measures of FEV1 and FVC were derived using the PDF Icon American Thoracic Society (ATS) criteria, firstly removing those values where the technique was judged not to be satisfactory then taking the maximum of the two qualifying measures at 53 years and the three qualifying measures at other ages. Impossible values were removed.

Data on air pollution were also collected as part of the 60–64 (2006-10) sweep.
