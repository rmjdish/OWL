Diabetes

Physician-diagnosed diabetes, HbA1c biomarker and metabolic syndrome data from age 31 to 79.

Diagnosis and data collection

Diabetes and age at onset were obtained from reports made at seven data collection waves at ages 36, 43, 53, 60–64, 68–70, 76–77 and 79, and from a postal questionnaire at 31 years. Type 1, Type 2 and gestational diabetes cases were distinguished based on age at diagnosis, medication history and documented reasons. The derived diabetes variables have been updated to take account of all the most recent data collections.

Biomarkers — HbA1c

Blood samples taken at 60–64, 68–70 and 77 years were tested for HbA1c, the most commonly-used biomarker for diabetes. Further details of blood sample collection are available in the biological samples section.

Metabolic syndrome

The metabolic syndrome at age 53 years and its components were defined using cut points recommended by ATPIII (2001), modified to include glycated haemoglobin (HbA1c) instead of fasting plasma glucose. Details of the cut points used are available on request.
