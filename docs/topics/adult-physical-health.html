Adult physical health

A wide range of physical health conditions and indicators tracked across the adult lifespan of NSHD study members.

Sub-topics

Sub-topicDescription
General healthSelf-rated health, ailment history, hospital admissions and the SF-36
Respiratory healthMRC respiratory questionnaire and lung function (FEV1, FVC, PEFR) from age 20 to 77
Cardiovascular healthBlood pressure, heart disease, angina, stroke, CVD risk factors and cardiological scans
DiabetesPhysician-diagnosed diabetes, HbA1c, metabolic syndrome and derived longitudinal variables
Physical capabilityGrip strength, chair rises, standing balance, walking speed and activities of daily living
Urinary incontinenceStress and urge incontinence data from age 48 to 77, including the ICIQ measure
MedicationBNF-categorised medication data at ages 36, 43, 53, 60–64, 68–70, 76–77 and 79
