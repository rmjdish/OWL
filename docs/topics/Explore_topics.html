Main topics

The main topics of data collection in the study are as follows. Please click through to see the more specific topics information covered by these main section headers:

TopicDescription
Childhood health and development Health, milestones, cognition, diet and parental information from childhood years
Adult physical health General health, respiratory, cardiovascular, diabetes, physical capability and more
Adult mental health and wellbeing Cognitive function, mental health and wellbeing across adulthood
Adult health behaviours Smoking, alcohol, diet, nutrition and physical activity
Anthropometry Birth weight, height, weight, body size and parental measurements across the lifespan
Lifetime social circumstances Education, employment, housing, marital history and social networks
Biological samples, DNA and structural assessments Blood, urine and saliva samples, DNA repository and structural assessments
Clinic visit – 60–64 years Intensive clinic data collection at age 60–64 covering cardiac, vascular, musculoskeletal, functional and biochemical assessments
Insight 46 – neuroscience sub-study Investigating how life course factors and genetics influence brain pathologies and dementia risk
