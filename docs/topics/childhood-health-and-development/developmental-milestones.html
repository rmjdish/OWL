Developmental milestones

Early physical and developmental milestones recorded by health visitors and school doctors between 1946 and 1957.

Overview

Health visitors made systematic observations of early developmental milestones during home visits in the first years of life (1946–1950). School medical examinations in 1952, 1954 and 1957 recorded additional physical and developmental information. Milestones recorded include motor development (sitting, standing, walking), speech, and developmental assessments made by health visitors and school doctors.

For detailed variable listings and naming conventions, please request data access via Skylark.
