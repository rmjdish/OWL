Childhood health

Maternal recall of childhood illness, infectious disease, accidents and hospital admissions from birth to age 25.

Childhood illness and infectious disease

Maternal recall of childhood illness and infectious disease was collected in 1948, 1950, 1952, 1954 and 1957. Mothers were asked about whooping cough, measles, scarlet fever and mumps, but more general information about colds was also collected.

Accidents and hospital admissions

Reports of accidents and maternal recall of hospital admissions were collected. Hospital admissions were classified into 15 categories (based on ICD-8 categories) for the age ranges 0–4, 5–10 and 11–15 years. A similar set of variables were derived for accidents. These illness and accident summary variables were also repeated for ages 16–20 and 21–25.

Chronic illness

A set of variables describing childhood chronic illness were derived in the 1970s.

Key publication

Pless IB and Douglas JWB (1971). Chronic illness in childhood: epidemiological and clinical characteristics. Pediatrics, 47, 405–414.
