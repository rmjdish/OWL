Questionnaires completed by study member

Questionnaires completed directly by NSHD study members across childhood, adulthood, and women-only surveys.

Questionnaires completed by study members during childhood, covering ages 8 to 15. At these waves study members took part in cognitive assessments including the Alice Heim (AH4) test, Watts-Vernon reading test, and mathematics test, and were also asked about social activities, occupation preferences, and employment ambitions.

📄 1954 (age 8) 📄 1957 (age 11) 📄 1961 (age 15)

Questionnaires completed by study members from early adulthood through to older age, covering ages 16 to 78. Topics ranged from employment, lifestyle, and family circumstances in early adult life through to health, cognition, and quality of life in later years.
