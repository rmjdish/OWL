Questionnaires completed by the school doctor

Topics covered by questionnaires completed by the school doctor, spanning four survey waves from 1952 to 1961. Surveys covered both medical history reported by the family and physical examinations carried out by the doctor.

ℹ️ Introduction

The school doctors examined the study members and collected information at four time points between the ages of six and 15. These surveys consisted of two parts: a medical history and an examination. In the medical history the school doctor asked the mother (or attending adult).

  • 1952 — First examination
  • 1953 — Second examination
  • 1957 — Third examination
  • 1961 — Final examination

A searchable word document of all the school doctor question texts can be downloaded here.
