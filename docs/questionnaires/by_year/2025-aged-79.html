2025 (aged 79)

NSHD survey wave.

👥 Responding Population

All eligible UK study members were contacted, as well as 25 study members who lived abroad. 1643 study members completed the questionnaire.

Provided information 1643
Cumulative total of deaths 1604
Cumulative permanent refusals (withdrawn from the study) 661
Currently not resident in England, Scotland or Wales 83
Failure to contact 899

📋 Data collection

The postal questionnaire was sent out when study members were aged 78. Reminders were sent out to all study members as part of the annual Birthday Card mailout.
