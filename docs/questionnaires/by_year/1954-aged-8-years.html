1954 (aged 8 years)

NSHD survey wave.

Responding Population

The mothers of all eligible study members were contacted: 4,435 mothers of cohort members were interviewed by the health visitor or school nurse.

Provided information 4,435
Cumulative total of deaths 236
Cumulative permanent refusals 1
Temporarily living abroad 40
Cumulative emigrated 259
Failure to contact 391
Total 5,362

Data collection

School nurses and health visitors interviewed mothers at home about the child's health and development, school absences, play, the mother's work, and the home and family circumstances. Teachers administered cognitive tests at school.

Training for data collection

Instructions are given at the head of each form.
