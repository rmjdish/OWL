1966-67 (aged 20-21 years)

NSHD survey wave.

Responding Population

In 1966 all eligible study members were contacted: 3,899 cohort members responded to the postal questionnaire.

Provided information 3,899
Cumulative total of deaths 268
Cumulative permanent refusals 7
Temporarily living abroad 39
Cumulative emigrated 333
Failure to contact 816
Total 5,362

Data collection

A postal questionnaire was sent to administrators at technical and further education establishments attended by cohort members to ask about course attended.

In this and other appropriate years a postal questionnaire was sent to university students in their final year to ask about courses attended, health and employment.

A postal questionnaire on marriage and fertility, health and employment was sent to all cohort members.

Training for data collection

Instructions are given at the head of each form.
