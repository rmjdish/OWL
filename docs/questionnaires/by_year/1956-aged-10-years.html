1956 (aged 10 years)

NSHD survey wave.

👥 Responding Population

The schools of all eligible study members were contacted.

Provided information 4,077
Cumulative total of deaths 240
Cumulative permanent refusals 1
Temporarily living abroad 37
Cumulative emigrated 273
Failure to contact 734
Total 5,362

📋 Data collection

The study member's teacher and the school's head teacher completed the postal questionnaire about the type of school, the child's attainment and behaviour, and parental interest.

🎓 Training for data collection

Instructions are given at the head of each form.

📇 Contents

  • Type of school
  • Type of class
  • Achievements and behaviour of the study member
  • Parents’ interest and home environment
