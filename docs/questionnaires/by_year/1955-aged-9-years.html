1955 (aged 9 years)

NSHD survey wave.

Responding Population

The mothers of all eligible study members were contacted: 4,181 mothers of cohort members were interviewed.

Provided information 4,181
Cumulative total of deaths 239
Cumulative permanent refusals 1
Temporarily living abroad 40
Cumulative emigrated 275
Failure to contact 626

Data collection

School nurses and health visitors interviewed mothers at home about the child's health, development and behaviour, school absences, the mother's work, and the home and family circumstances; they also commented on the state of the dwelling.

Training for data collection

Instructions are given at the head of each form.
