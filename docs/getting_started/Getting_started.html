The National Survey of Health and Development is Britain's longest-running birth cohort study, following over 5,000 people born in a single week of March 1946 from birth into their eighties. Researchers have returned to the same individuals repeatedly over 80 years of sweeps, collecting data on physical health, cognitive development, mental wellbeing, lifestyle, and socioeconomic circumstance.

The OWL turns that archive of thousands of variables into something navigable. The pages below will get you oriented, however you're approaching the site.
