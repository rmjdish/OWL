Getting started

New to the NSHD or the OWL resource? Start here for orientation, then dig into whichever page below answers your question.

The National Survey of Health and Development (NSHD) is Britain's longest-running birth cohort study, following over 5,000 people born in a single week of March 1946 from birth into their eighties. Researchers have returned to the same individuals repeatedly across nearly 80 years — referred to as sweeps — collecting information on physical health, cognitive development, mental wellbeing, lifestyle, and socioeconomic circumstances.

The OWL exists to make that decades-deep archive navigable. The three pages below will get you oriented, however you're approaching the site.
