Documentation - I46P2_History- Insight46 phase 2

| Categories of variables: (may be more than one) | Family and Medical History | | Summary of work undertaken | Cleaning Description/Rationale: Downloaded from XNAT Dropped all variables containing no data Labelled values for multiple-choice options according to the questionnaire (see below) Labelled missing data (see below) -99 (missing for unknown reason) -88 Not applicable -77 Still alive -66 Unknown | Missing and excluded data were coded as -99 – this applies to all values that were missing due to the participant not completing a certain measure. Where values were blank due to the variable being not applicable to that participant, they are coded as -88. For example is a participants response no to question 1, since the last visit have you had a loss of consciousness etc. then subsequent questions are not asked. For the death variables (mother, father & siblings) if the family member is still alive, this is coded as -77. For the age of onset and age at death variables, where the question was asked to the participant but the answer was unknown by the participant, this is coded as -66.*

On the paper XNAT forms, the codes ‘Unknown’ 99, ‘NA’ 88 and ‘Still alive’ 77 were used for the age of death variables and for the age of onset variables the codes ‘Unknown’ 99, ‘NA 88’ were used. As these values could be valid ages and therefore their meaning ambiguous, all ages 99, 88 & 77 were checked against the paper records and Phase 1 data to verify the correct answer, where this remained ambiguous the responses were coded as unknown, now -66.
