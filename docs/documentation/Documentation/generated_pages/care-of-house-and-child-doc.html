Other socioeconomic circumstances variables descriptions

Information was provided by the mothers at home interviews taken by the health visitor in 1950.

Care of house and child in 1950

**Raw variables ** (they are not included in the derived dataset)

CLH50 – Cleanliness of home? 1 = Average 2 = Among the most clean 3 = Among the least clean

CLC50 – Cleanliness of child? 1 = Average 2 = Among the most clean 3 = Among the least clean

MAN50 – Mother’s management of child compared with the standard among other mother’s in your care? 1 = Average 2 = Among the best 3 = Among the worst

SHO50 – Are this child’s shoes of satisfactory size and type? 1 = Satisfactory 2 = Unsatisfactory

CLO50 – Is this child’s clothing in a satisfactory or unsatisfactory state of repair? 1 = Satisfactory 2 = Unsatisfactory

The red categories were collapsed together and coded as 0 The blue categories were collapsed together and were coded as 1

A score of care of the home and child (range 0-5) allocated one point for each of the following ratings by the health visitor: very clean house, very clean child, at least adequate shoes, at least adequate clothes, mother coped well. Those who scored 0-2 and those scoring 3 or 4 were compared with those scoring the highest (the best care).

CHILC50X – Care of the house and child in 1950

0 = Best 1 = Intermediate 2 = Worst

Housing quality in 1950

**Raw variables ** (they are not included in the derived dataset)

REP50 – Assessment of the state of repair of dwelling? 1 = Average 2 = Very good 3 = Bad

DWED50 – Cleanliness of child? 1 = Built before 1919 2 = Built 1919 - 1939 3 = Built since 1939

CROW50 – Crowing 1954 1 - 1/2 person per room 2 - 1 person per room 3 - 1 1/2 person per room 4 - 2 person per room 5 - 2 1/2 person per room 6 - 3 person per room 7 - 3 1/2 person per room 8 - 3 1/2 + person per room

The red categories were collapsed together and coded as 0 The blue categories were collapsed together and were coded as 1

A score of the housing quality (range 0-3) allocated one point for each of the following: dwelling in very good repair, dwelling built since 1919, no overcrowding (no more than one and a half persons per room). Those who scored 0 and those scoring 1 were compared with those scoring 2 or 3 (the best conditions).

LIVC50X – Housing quality in 1950

0 = Best 1 = Intermediate 2 = Worst
