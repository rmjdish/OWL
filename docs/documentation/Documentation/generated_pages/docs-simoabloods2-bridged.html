Documentation - I46P2_SIMOABLOODS2– Insight46 Wave 2 For Submission to the NSHD Scientific Support Team | Name of person responsible for cleaning/derivation: | Dylan Williams, dylan.williams@ucl.ac.uk Ashvini Keshavan, a.keshavan@ucl.ac.uk | | Date of submitting documentation | August 2024 | | Categories of variables*: (may be more than one) | Blood, urine and other samples | | Summary of work undertaken | Category: Derivation Description: Overview The returned data are measures of a blood-based biomarker for Alzheimer’s disease neuropathology called p-tau 217. Plasma samples collected at 69-year home visits were assayed using the ALZpath Simoa p-tau 217 assay version 2 at the UK DRI Biomarker Factory at UCL in spring 2024. 952 samples were assayed: this subset of approximately half of all available 69-year home visit plasma samples was selected to prioritise assaying of samples from participants who had not enrolled in Insight 46 phases 1 and 2 (in which plasma p-tau 217 had already been measured). In samples from those who had not attended Insight 46 phases 1 and 2, there was also preferential selection for individuals who had died following the 69-year home visits, in order to ascertain p-tau 217 concentrations for individuals who would have no prospect of joining Insight 46 during phase 3 (where p-tau 217 assaying is planned). Assay result adjustments We provide three adjustments to the raw p-tau 217 assay results: i) reassignment of values under the lower limit of quantification (0.06 pg/ml) to 0.06pg/ml; ii) adjustment of values from (i) for assay variation across plates; iii) adjustment of values from (ii) to bridge from the testing of 69-year home visit samples to the testing of plasma p-tau 217 values from Insight 46, also undertaken with the ALZpath Simoa assay (version 1 or 2?) As part of the assaying of p-tau 217 on 69-year home visit samples, 18 plasma samples from Insight 46 were also assayed to allow for bridging of these new data to former p-tau 217 values generated on Insight 46 samples. 2 pooled plasma control samples (also used in the previous Insight 46 assaying) were also included in the assaying. Together, these 20 plasma samples were used to calculate bridging of the 69-year home visit assay results to Insight 46 assay results. The values from the 18 individual bridging samples were all from singlicate analysis in Insight46 (adjusted) and were the means of duplicate analysis in NSHD (adjusted). The values from the pooled plasma controls were means calculated from across all the 5 runs in Insight46 and up to 16 plates in NSHD. 2 pooled CSF controls were also plated across each of the 16 plates and were used to derive the plate-specific internal adjustment factor. They had also previously been quantified in Insight 46 but they were not used for the bridging calculation because they were manually diluted with different dilutions in Insight46 vs NSHD, and although they were very similar once adjusted for dilution, the dilution-adjusted concentrations are in a different range and would unduly influence correction based on linear regression if applied. For bridging, a univariable linear regression model was applied to derive the conversion factor as follows: y = bx where y = Insight46 value, x = NSHD value and b = bridging factor. The constant was fixed at zero in this model, as zero should hold true across both quantifications given that it is the same assay.
