Summary of work undertakenCleaning / Derivation / **Both**

Cleaning:

- Data was extracted from XNAT

- Any missing data was filled in manually using original paper copies of questionnaires where available

- Any remaining missing values were coded as -99

Derivation:

- Age at questionnaire was derived in Excel using date of birth and date of study visit
Names of source variablesSubject, M/F, stateAnxiety, traitAnxiety, gumDisease, selfRatedTeethAndGumHealth, treatmentForGumDisease, toothLossWithoutInjury, lostBoneAroundTeeth, toothNotLookingRight, flossedInLastSevenDays, rinsedInLastSevenDays, actOutDreams, totalHandedness
Output variablesgumdisease_i46p1, selfdentalhealth_i46p1, gumdiseasetreatment_i46p1, toothloss_i46p1, lostbonearoundteeth_i46p1, toothnotlookingright_i46p1, flossing_i46p1, rinsing_i46p1, actoutdreams_i46p1, totalhandedness_i46p1, traitanxietyrec_i46p1, stateanxietyrec_i46p1

Codings

  • subject – participant ID number
  • visitdate – date of study visit (yearmonthdate)
  • sex – male=0, female=1
  • stateanxiety – total score out of 80
  • traitanxiety – total score out of 80
  • gumdisease – do you think you might have gum disease? – no=0, yes=1, don’t know=9
  • selfdentalhealth – overall, how would you rate the health of your teeth and gums? – excellent=1, very good=2, good=3, fair=4, poor=5, don’t know=6
  • gumdiseasetreatment – have you ever had treatment for gum disease such as scaling and root planning, sometimes called “deep cleaning”? – no=0, yes=1, don’t know=9
  • toothloss – have you ever had any teeth become loose on their own, without an injury? – no=0, yes=1, don’t know=9
  • lostbonearoundteeth – have you ever been told by a dental professional that you lost bone around your teeth? – no=0, yes=1, don’t know=9
  • toothnotlookingright – during the past three months, have you noticed a tooth that doesn’t look right? – no=0, yes=1, don’t know=9
  • flossing – aside from brushing your teeth with a toothbrush, in the last seven days, how many times did you use dental floss or any other device to clean between your teeth? – number of days, range 0-7
  • rinsing – aside from brushing your teeth with a toothbrush, in the last seven days, how many times did you use mouthwash or other dental rinse product that you use to treat dental disease or dental problems? – number of days, range 0-7
  • actoutdreams – have you ever been told, or suspected yourself, that you seem to ‘act out your dreams’ while asleep (for example, punching, flailing your arms in the air, making running movements, etc)? – no=0, yes=1, don’t know=9
  • totalhandedness – hand preference, range -24(left) to 24(right)
