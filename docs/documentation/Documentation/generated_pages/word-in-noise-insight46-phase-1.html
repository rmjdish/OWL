Alterations of NAWM integrity can be assessed by diffusion tensor imaging (DTI) which enables sensitive estimation of white matter tissue microstructure environments using water diffusion properties. Parameters typically include fractional anisotropy (FA), a measure of directionality, and mean diffusivity (MD), a measure of magnitude of diffusion. Additional multicompartmental modelling techniques have been developed to address limitations in these conventional measures. For example, neurite orientation dispersion and density imaging (NODDI) estimates microstructural complexity parameters of neurite density index (NDI), a measure of fraction of axons and dendrites; and orientation dispersion index (ODI), a measure of variability of neurite orientation (5). Lower values for FA and NDI and higher values for MD and ODI are thought to represent worse microstructural integrity.
