Overview

Social Class: 1999

| Description of variables | Information about own and partner’s occupation was asked at 26, 36, 43 and 53 years. Information about the occupation of the father was asked at many of the follow-ups in childhood. Occupation was initially coded using the Registrar General occupational classification at the time. In 1976, the research team retrospectively applied the 1970 OPCS Classification of Occupations to code the current or last occupation and employment status of the fathers (or of the current husband) and derived the socioeconomic group and social class in 1950, 1957 and 1961 when the Study members were aged 4, 11 and 15 years respectively. | | Summary of work undertaken | Recoding, derivation and labelling of occupation and social class variables at age 53: Current or most recent own social class (SCR99U) Current or most recent social class of spouse (SPSCR99U) HOH’s social class (SC53U) Overall social class of SM 15-53 (SC1553) Overall social class of spouse (SPSC1553). | | Output variables (please list names of new variables created) | SCR99U,SC53U,SPSCR99U,SC1553,SPSC1553 |
