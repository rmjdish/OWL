Life stress variables

LFSTR94-LFSTR00: Life stress The life stress variables are a cumulative score with a minimum allowed range of 0 and a maximum of 16. Score 1 for each of the following: Family life got a little or a lot worse in last year Work life got a little or a lot worse in last year Serious difficulties with spouse in last year Serious difficulties with children in last year Serious difficulties with parents or other relatives in last year Serious difficulties at work in last year Reports (in Q4) that something has made life worse in the last year (one point each for : worries to do with own health, other worries about self, worries about spouse, worries about children, worries about parents, worries about work, other worries) Reports goes without things really needed because short of money (score 1 for sometimes and 2 for often)

-FMSTR00: Family stress variables only: Family life got a little or a lot worse in last year Serious difficulties with spouse in last year Serious difficulties with children in last year Serious difficulties with parents or other relatives in last year Reports (in Q4) that something has made life worse in the last year (one point each for : worries about spouse, worries about children, worries about parents) Reports goes without things really needed because short of money (score 1 for sometimes and 2 for often)

WKSTR94-WKSTR00: Work stress Work life got a little or a lot worse in last year Serious difficulties at work in last year Reports (in Q4) that something has made life worse in the last year (one point each for : worries about work) Reports goes without things really needed because short of money (score 1 for sometimes and 2 for often)
