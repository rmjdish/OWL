Methods Details of the commercially available Lumipulse Aβ40, Aβ42, p-tau181 and t-tau assays are given in the kit inserts and they were all used as per the manufacturer’s instructions. All measurements were conducted on the same Lumipulse G600II platform (Fujirebio) at the Neuroimmunology Clinical Laboratory, National Hospital for Neurology and Neurosurgery, London, UK. The Lumipulse assays required 100 μL of dead volume, 50 μL/replicate for measuring Aβ42, 40 μL/replicate for measuring Aβ40, 40 μL/replicate for measuring p-tau181 and 75 μL/replicate for measuring t-tau. Measurements were undertaken in singlicate, using two reagent batches: the first 72 samples were assayed in 2019 for an interim analysis (1) and the remaining 62 samples were assayed in 2021. For the first batch, one 500 μL aliquot of neat CSF was used to measure Aβ42 and Aβ40 on a single thaw on 03/05/2019; a separate 500 μL aliquot of neat CSF was used to measure p-tau181 and t-tau on a single thaw on 06/06/2019. For the second batch, one 500 μL aliquot of neat CSF was used to measure all four analytes on 05/05/2021 and 16/06/2021 on a single thaw.

After the measurements of Aβ peptides were performed for batch 1, certified reference materials (CRM) were produced for Aβ1-42 and data have been published on the commutability of different assay platforms in measuring against these CRM (2). The values obtained by measuring Aβ42 on the Lumipulse platform were retrospectively adjusted to incorporate standardization against the CRM, using information kindly provided by Dr Nathalie Le Bastard (Fujirebio) (3). For batch 2, the values of the Aβ42 calibrators were already specified against the CRM. For each of the four analytes, three quality control samples supplied with the assay kits (low, medium and high) were included on each batch run; the values of these were within the ranges specified in the kit inserts.
