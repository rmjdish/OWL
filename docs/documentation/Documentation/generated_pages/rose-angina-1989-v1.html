Rose Angina 1989

There are 3 scales for Rose Angina: stage 1, stage 2 and a combined variable.

Stage 1 Rose Angina

If Study member has ever had any pain or discomfort in their chest (ANGIN89 =1)

The all of the following conditions must be met:

The pain must be in the Sternum (upper and middle) or Sternum (lower), left anterior chest or the left arm; (CHPRL89 <=4)

They must get the pain when walking uphill or in a hurry but not when walking at an ordinary pace on the level; (CHPRU89 = 1 AND CHPRN89 = 0)

That pain is relieved in 10 minutes or less when they stand still. (CHPRS89 = 1 AND CHPRT89 = 1).

Stage 2 Angina

If Study member has ever had any pain or discomfort in their chest (ANGIN89 =1)

The all of the following conditions must be met:

The pain must be in the Sternum (upper and middle) or Sternum (lower), left anterior chest or the left arm; (CHPRL89 <=4)

They must get the pain when walking at an ordinary pace on the level; (CHPRn89 = 1)

That pain is relieved in 10 minutes or less when they stand still. (CHPRS89 = 1 AND CHPRT89 = 1).

Overall Rose Angina

This is the sum of stage 1 and 2.

Study members who did not answer the routing question (ever had any pain or discomfort in their chest) were coded as unknown unless they answered the rest of the battery of questions.

Study members who did not answer the walking uphill AND walking on the flat were also coded as ‘unknown’.

Otherwise respondents who answered most but not all of the questions were coded as ‘no’.
