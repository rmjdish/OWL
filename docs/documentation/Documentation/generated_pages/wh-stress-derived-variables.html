Sarah Tom Created: 09 September 2008 Updated: 24 September 2008 Derived Variables

Work Stress

During the 1993-2000 annual women’s health questionnaires, study members responded to questions concerning potential sources of stress related to work in the past 12 months: whether the respondent had to go without things really needed because she was short of money; serious difficulties at work.

Raw Variables

The work stress variables in year yy, for years 1993-2000.

GWINCyy – Had to go without things you really needed in the last 12 months because you were short of money (response of no = 0; yes, sometimes = 1; yes, often = 2)

WKDFyy – In the last 12 months have you had serious difficulties at work? (response of no = 0; yes = 1)

Derived Variables

WKSTRyy – Is the sum of responses to the two questions, ranging from 0 – 3.

Family Stress

During the 1993-2000 annual women’s health questionnaires, study members responded to questions concerning potential sources of stress related to family in the past 12 months.

Raw Variables

The family stress variables in year yy, for years 1993-2000.

PACLyy – In the last 12 months have you had a parent, parent-in-law (or other elderly relative) come and live with you? (response of no = 0; yes = 1)

SPDFyy – In the last 12 months have you had serious difficulties with your spouse/partner because of their health, behaviour, or other reasons? (response of no = 0; yes = 1)

CHDFyy – In the last 12 months have you had serious difficulties with any of your children because of their health, behaviour, or other reasons? (response of no = 0; yes = 1)

PADFyy – In the last 12 months have you had serious difficulties with your parents, parents-in-law (or other relatives) because of their health, behaviour, or for other reasons? (response of no = 0; yes = 1)

Derived Variables

FMSTRyy – Is the sum of responses to the four questions, with a score ranging from 0 – 4.

Life Stress

This variable is a combination of responses for work stress and family stress over years 1993 – 2000.

Raw Variables

The work stress variables in year yy, for years 1993-2000.

GWINCyy – Had to go without things you really needed in the last 12 months because you were short of money (response of no = 0; yes, sometimes = 1; yes, often = 2)

WKDFyy – In the last 12 months have you had serious difficulties at work? (response of no = 0; yes = 1)

PACLyy – In the last 12 months have you had a parent, parent-in-law (or other elderly relative) come and live with you? (response of no = 0; yes = 1)

SPDFyy – In the last 12 months have you had serious difficulties with your spouse/partner because of their health, behaviour, or other reasons? (response of no = 0; yes = 1)

CHDFyy – In the last 12 months have you had serious difficulties with any of your children because of their health, behaviour, or other reasons? (response of no = 0; yes = 1)

PADFyy – In the last 12 months have you had serious difficulties with your parents, parents-in-law (or other relatives) because of their health, behaviour, or for other reasons? (response of no = 0; yes = 1)

Derived Variables

LFSTRyy – Is the sum of responses to questions concerning work and life stress, ranging from 0 – 6.
