Overview

Documentation – I46P2_AD8 – Insight46 wave 2

| Categories of variables*: (may be more than one) | AD8 Informant Questionnaire | | Summary of work undertaken | Cleaning Description/Rationale: Data downloaded from XNAT Description/Rationale: Not applicable variables codes as -88 Values labelled according to the multiple-choice options in the questionnaire (see below) |

Where values were blank due to the variable being not applicable to that participant, they are coded as -88. For example reason why not completed (ad8wn) is not asked if informant questionnaire completed. The AD8 is a short interview designed to be administered to a participant’s informant to distinguish those with very mild dementia from those without dementia. In Insight 46 it was given in parallel to the MyCog questionnaire. A validity study demonstrated strong correlation between AD8 scores, Clinical Dementia Rating domains, and performance on neuropsychological tests in 255 patient—informant dyads. Galvin, J.E., C.M. Roe, C. Xiong, and J.C. Morris. 2006. Validity and reliability of the AD8 informant interview in dementia. Neurology. 67:1942-1948.
