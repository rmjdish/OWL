Education beyond 26

Using data collected at age 36 yrs and 43 yrs variables have been created to capture any further education undertaken by study members. Both job-related and independently gained qualifications have been included.

FQUAL82, FQAUL89

Purpose : Qualifications gained as a result of training at 36 yrs, 43 yrs Year : 1982, 1989

1. Definition 0 None 1 Qualifications up to ‘O’ level or equivalent 2 Qualifications up to ‘A’ level or equivalent or beyond -999 No questionnaire 88 Na, no courses or examinations taken 99/9 Unknown

2. Specification

RECODE tqual82, tqual89 (0=0) (1 thru 3=1) (4 thru 8=2) (ELSE=COPY) into fqual82, fqual89.

EDU82, EDU89

Purpose : Training and qualifications at 36 yrs, 43 yrs Year : 1982, 1989

1. Definition 0 No training 1 Training but no quals 2 Training and quals up to 'O' level or equivalent 3 Training and quals at 'A' level or equivalent or beyond 4 No training and quals up to 'O' level or equivalent 5 No training and quals at 'A' level or equivalent or beyond

2. Specification

if(train82=0) edu82=0. if(train82=1 and fqual82=0) edu82=1. if(train82=1 and fqual82=1) edu82=2. if(train82=1 and fqual82=2) edu82=3. * There are 56 people have not had any training but are fqual82=2 (A level +). if(train82=0 and fqual82=2) edu82=5.

if(train89=0) edu89=0. if(train89=1 and fqual89=0) edu89=1. if(train89=1 and fqual89=1) edu89=2. if(train89=1 and fqual89=2) edu89=3. * There are 11 people have not had any training but are fqual89=1 (O level +). if(train89=0 and fqual89=1) edu89=4. * There are 4 people have not had any training but are fqual82=2 (A level +). if(train89=0 and fqual89=2) edu89=5.

FURTHED

Purpose : Further education since age 26 Year : 1982, 1989

1. Definition 0 None 1 Evening/adult education only 2 Qualifications up to 'O' level or equivalent 3 Qualifications at 'A' level or equivalent or beyond

2. Specification

if(edu82=0 and edu89=0) furthed=0. if(edu82 gt 0 or adec82=1 or adecr89=1 or edu89 gt 0) furthed=1. if(any(edu82,2,4) or any(edu89,2,4)) furthed=2. if(any(edu82,3,5) or any(edu89,3,5)) furthed=3.

FUEDGP

Purpose : Further education since age 26 - grouped Year : 1982, 1989

1. Definition 0 No quals/evening classes/adult educ only 1 Quals at 'O' level and beyond

2. Specification

Collapses furthed* into ‘None/adult/eveing clases only’ vs ‘any quals beyond 'O' level’. Recode furthed (0=0) (1=0) (2=1) (3=1) into fuedgp

FEDGP

Purpose : Any education beyond age 26 Year : 1982, 1989

1. Definition 0 None 1 Any education beyond 26

2. Specification

Collapses furthed* into ‘none’ vs ‘any education beyond age 26’ - fedgp. Recode furthed (0=0) (1 thru 3=1) into fedgp
