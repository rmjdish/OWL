Summary

Vascular medication outcome variables at 69-71y (Phase 1 Insight 46) (2019-CL)

This document details vascular medication variables derived from Insight 46 data. All participants had medication history (usage within the last 24 hours, including over the counter medication) collected in person by a Clinical Research Associate. All participants had been advised to bring a list of their medication to their study visit. Generic or trade names were recorded, depending on what was provided. Antihypertensive medication, diabetic and lipid-regulating medication usage was then coded by CL. In the case of antihypertensive medication, a coding system based on BNF groups was used, originally derived by Mark Rawle. It should be noted that information from all medication groups is required to capture all potential anti-hypertensive medications and therefore an additional variable was derived (bploweringmed_bin_70). There was no missing data.

Variables

(Please not original variables are used. See below for updated variable names)

medicationusage_70 – whether any medication used (not limited to vascular medication

Coded as:

0 = no medication 1 = medication used

medhtn_70 - Presence of Antihypertensive medication. These drugs are prescribed for high blood pressure or heart failure and includes angiotensin converting enzyme inhibitors (BNF255). Includes BNF groups 2.5.1, 2.5.2, 2.5.3, 2.5.4, 2.5.5, 2.5.5.1, 2.5.5.2 & 2.5.5.3.   meddiuretic_70 - Presence of Diuretics. These drugs may be prescribed to promote renal function and offload fluid (for example in heart failure) or to lower blood pressure. The group includes thiazide like diuretics (BNF221), loop diuretics (BNF222), potassium sparing diuretics (BNF223, BNF224) and other diuretics. Includes BNF groups 2.2.1, 2.2.2, 2.2.3, 2.2.4, 2.2.5, 2.2.6, 2.2.7 & 2.2.8.

medbeta_70 - Presence of Beta Blockers. These drugs may be prescribed for heart failure, tachycardia, anxiety, hypertension or ischaemic heart disease. Includes BNF group 2.4.

mednitrateccb_70 - Presence of Nitrates. These drugs may be prescribed to lower blood pressure, or in heart failure or ischaemic heart disease. It includes calcium channel blockers (BNF262). Includes BNF groups 2.6.1, 2.6.2, 2.6.3 & 2.6.4.

bploweringmed_bin_70 – Presence of any potential blood pressure lowering medication. Derived from medhtn_70, meddiuretic_70, medbeta_70 and mednitrateccb_70.

All coded as:

0 = no medication 1 = presence of medication

bploweringmed_total70 – Total number of potential antihypertensive medications being taken. Derived from original data. dmmed70 – Total number of diabetic medications being taken. Based on BNF groups 6.1.1 (insulins) and 6.1.2 (oral antidiabetic drugs). diabeticmed_bin_70 – Presence of diabetic medication. Binary variable derived from dmmed70. Based on BNF groups 6.1.1 (insulins) and 6.1.2 (oral antidiabetic drugs). cholmed70 – Total number of lipid-regulating medications being taken. Based on BNF group 2.12. cholmed_bin_70 – Presence of lipid-regulating medication. Binary variable derived from cholmed70. Based on BNF group 2.12.
