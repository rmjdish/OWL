Respiratory disease up to 2014

In 1966, 1971, 1977, 1982, 1989, 1999, 2009 and 2014 (ages 20, 25, 31, 36, 43, 53, 60-64, 68) study members were asked the MRC respiratory questions. In 1999 a variable [respn99] was derived indicating whether the SM suffered from respiratory disease according to their answers to these set questions. Comparable variables [respn09, respn14] were derived in 2009 and 2014 indicating whether the study member had symptoms of respiratory disease or not.

respn99, respn09, respn14

Purpose : Respiratory disease at age 53yrs Year : 1999, 2009, 2014

1. Definition 0 No resp problems/No symptoms 1 Resp problems/Symptoms 8 No interview 9 Unknown/Missing

CROSSTABS PHLC BY PHLDC BY PHLMC DO IF (WIMC EQ 1 OR PHLMC EQ 1 OR WZYDC EQ 1 OR NUMC EQ 2) COMPUTE RESPN99=1 ELSE IF (WIMC EQ 9 OR PHLMC EQ 9 OR WZYDC EQ 9 OR NUMC EQ 9) COMPUTE RESPN99=9 ELSE IF (Q99A EQ 0) COMPUTE RESPN99=8 ELSE IF (Q99A EQ 1) COMPUTE RESPN99=0
END IF
