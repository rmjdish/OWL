Prior Derivation (Previously performed by W. Coath)

Brain age values were calculated on the UCL Centre for Medical Image Computing Computer Science cluster using version 1 of the ‘BrainAgeR’ software (Cole et al., 2019 doi: 10.31219/osf.io/3b6zu).

Brain-predicted age was produced for all Insight 46 Phase 1 participants with a T1 MRI scan which passed quality control.

The brn_bpa_i46p1 variable is the brain-predicted age after adjustment for age bias. The age bias adjustment was performed by subtracting 3.33 from the unadjusted brain age and dividing by 0.91. These values were the intercept and slope of the model in the training dataset of brain-predicted age regressed on chronological age.

Brain-predicted age difference (Brain-PAD) is provided for the adjusted brain-predicted age estimates only. This was calculated by subtracting chronological age from adjusted brain-predicted age.
