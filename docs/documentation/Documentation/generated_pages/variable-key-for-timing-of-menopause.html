Documentation Gm updated by Bl

This includes derived variables which incorporates the information provided by the women annually in the postal questionnaires up to 2000, a brief questionnaire in 2003 OR by completing a questionnaire during the home visit in 1999.

(Of the N = 1735 women who responded to at least one postal questionnaire or to the short brief questionnaire in 2003 plus women who did not return any of the postal questionnaires but answered a questionnaire during the home visit, N= 1638 provided enough information to calculated the timing of menopause variables for survival/competing risk analysis).
