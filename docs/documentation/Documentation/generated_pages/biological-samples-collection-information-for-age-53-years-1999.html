Data collection methods

Data collection was carried out at home visits to cohort members by research nurses recruited and employed by the National Centre for Social Research. An electronic questionnaire was administered by nurses using laptop computers. Nurses took a buccal swab from which DNA was derived. Nurses also took non- fasting blood samples for DNA, cardiovascular risk factors (cholesterol, HDL cholesterol, triglyceride, glycosolated haemoglobin), [ a haemoglobin, red and white cell counts] and nutritional markers (red cell folate, plasma folate, plasma B12, plasma ferritin). Samples were posted to laboratories for analysis. The cholesterol, HDL cholesterol, triglyceride, and glycosylated haemoglobin were measured at the Royal Victoria Hospital in Newcastle England in 2000. Total cholesterol was measured by enzymatic CHOD-PAP. Precipitation for measurement of HDL cholesterol was performed using phosphotungstic Mg 2+ ; triglycerides were measured using a glycerol/kinase POD-linked reaction of glycerol liberated enzymatically from triglycerides. All of these measurements were made with a Bayer DAX-72. LDL cholesterol was calculated using the Friedwald formula; LDL cholesterol= total cholesterol - HDL cholesterol - 0.45 x triglycerides. HbA1c was measured using an ion exchange method on a Tosoh A1c 2.2 analyser

The blood counts (haemoglobin, red and white cell counts) and nutritional markers (red cell folate, plasma folate, plasma B12, plasma ferritin) were done at RVI. The methods used were as follows: Hb – standard spectrophotometric analysis on a Coulter Electronics STKS analyzer.Red Cell folate / plasma folate /B12 and ferritin – all measured using specific immunoassays on an Abbott Imx analyzer.   The residual blood went to Dallas for DNA, and she logged the identifiers. She transferred blood to blood transfusion that did blood typing and sent the residual to ECACC (according to Warren Hilder). Dallas says no blood went to ICH in 1999. She has forwarded query to Lynne and I await the result. The buccal samples also were sent to UCL (Prof Dallas Swallow at UCL). Full details are in the nurse manual at I:\LHA\Questionnaires.

Results

Following results files were received.

  • RVI sent us the results in excel spreads sheets.
  • RVI gave people SEPR numbers (SSN numbers).
  • Data came back in 3-4 data files with SSN numbers which were converted into SPSS files with our serial numbers in them. These SPSS files were nor converted into a library file.
