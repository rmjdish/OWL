Variable Key for breast cancer survival (up to Dec 2008)

Author of update GM 21/04/09

Of the 2547 women who had been in the birth cohort, 2253 women were eligible for the analyses. Of the 294 who were excluded, 284 had died or emigrated before 1971, 10 could not be flagged.

These variables have been updated to the end of December 2008. There were (n = 97) breast cancer cases. Follow-up was analysed from 1st January 1971, when the women were aged 24 years and 9 months.

These variables are: BRCA08 BCCENS FUPM BCDTM

BRCA08 breast cancer status up to end of Dec 08

This variable is an updated version (to end of Dec 08) of the variable BRCA05 (which was only up to end of Sept 2005). The value labels are given below.

| 0 | = | No | | 1 | = | Yes | | 99 | = | unknown | | 99999 | = | Non-respondent/ not in eligible sample |

BCCENS Br cancer status for survival analysis up to end of Dec08

This variable has been updated (to end of Dec 08). The previous version was up to end of Set 2005. This variable is for use in survival analysis where you are interested in a case / not a case in the eligible sample; hence the unknown status were coded as no.The value labels are given below.

| 0 | = | No | | 1 | = | Yes | | 99999 | = | Non respondent/ not in eligible sample/men |

**FUPM ** Follow-up time in months since 1st Jan 1971

This variable has been updated (to end of Dec 08) and gives the follow-up time in the following order: breast cancer diagnosis, last contact, date of emigration, date of death, date of last cancer registration. This is a continuous variable with unknown/not appropriate is coded =99999

**BCDTM ** Follow-up time in month since birth This variable has been updated (to end of Dec08) and gives the follow-up time in the following order: breast cancer diagnosis, last contact, date of emigration, date of death, date of last cancer registration. This is a continuous variable with unknown/not appropriate is coded =99999
