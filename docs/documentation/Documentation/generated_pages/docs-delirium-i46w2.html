| Categories of variables*: (may be more than one) | Delirium (Insight) | | Summary of work undertaken | Cleaning / Derivation / Both± Description/Rationale: Cleaning of delirium data to ensure missing cases accurately recorded. Derivation of delirium variables to achieve parity with existing delirium variables in NSHD (including presence of delirium, reported cause of delirium and duration of delirium). Delirium was defined by a positive answer to one or more questions related to the DSM-IV criteria (i.e. symptoms, cause of delirium, duration of delirium or year of delirium onset). Separate variables created for informant versus participant reported delirium (may be useful for future sensitivity analyses). |

Delirium data from Insight46 Phase 2 (2022 MJR)

This document details all the variables created after cleaning (provided in attached syntax file) on the delirium variables in the raw Phase 2 Insight46 data.

1) Delirium reporting (Participant)

During the neuroimaging substudy data collection, participants were asked a short series of questions on delirium. If willing and able, participants were asked a series of four questions over two minutes, related to DSM-IV criteria for delirium diagnosis.

2) Delirium reporting (Informant)

In addition to the participant questions outlined above, a further five-minute interview was conducted with a designated informant (where available). These were again related to DSM-IV criteria for delirium diagnosis. All participant and informant questions are detailed in full in ‘Delirium in NSHD and Insight46.docx’ compiled by Prof Daniel Davis in Oct 2014.

Variables:

delir_i46p2 – Presence of delirium, reported by participant or informant. Note: any positive value for any of the other variables included below will flag as a positive case of delirium, as per DSM-IV diagnostic criteria. delirp_i46p2– Presence of delirium, reported by participant.
Note: any positive value for any of the participant symptom questions, or the participant related variables included below will flag as a positive case of delirium, as per DSM-IV diagnostic criteria. deliri_i46p2 – Presence of delirium, reported by informant.
Note: any positive value for any of the informant symptom questions, or the informant related variables included below will flag as a positive case of delirium, as per DSM-IV diagnostic criteria. delircm_i46p2 – Presence of delirium caused by medication use, reported by participant or informant. delirci_i46p2 – Presence of delirium caused by infection, reported by participant or informant. delirco_i46p2 – Presence of delirium caused by surgical operation, reported by participant or informant. delircp_i46p2 – Presence of delirium caused by pain, reported by participant or informant. delircoth_i46p2 – Presence of delirium due to any other cause not included above, reported by participant. Causes provided in free text responses listed in variable illnessOther. For all of the variables above, values are coded as: 0 = No 1 = Yes NA = Missing (no section of test completed and reason for this unknown)

delird_i46p2 – Duration of delirium (note informant delirDI and participant delirDP values displayed 100% parity, so were combined into a single variable). For the variables above, values are coded as: 0 = No delirium reported 1 = Duration of 1-2 days 2 = Duration of 3-7 days 3 = Duration of 1-2 weeks 4 = Duration of 2 or more weeks NA = Missing (no section of test completed and reason for this unknown)

deliryp_i46p2 – Year of delirium onset, provided by participant. deliryi_i46p2 – Year of delirium onset, provided by informant. For the variables above, values are coded as: XXXX = Numerical value of year 0 = No year provided NA = Missing (no section of test completed and reason for this unknown)
