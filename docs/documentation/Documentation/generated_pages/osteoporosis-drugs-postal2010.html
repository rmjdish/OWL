Osteoporosis drugs for 2006-10 postal questionnaire

Postal questionnaires for 2006/10 were used – the coding looks at overall response to reasons for taking drugs and overall drugs that could be taken for osteoporosis; it doesn’t not mean that they said they took a specific drug for osteoporosis.

  1. oestod This binary variable just indicates if the reason for taking any drugs under Q24 was osteoporosis. The codes taken as indicative were M810, M811, M812, M813, M814, M815, M816, M817, M818, M819.
