Derived library – Waist and hip circumference

Waist and hip circumference were measured twice at age 43 (ABC189, ABC289; HIPC189, HIPC289) and once at age 53 (ABC99, HIPC99). Waist to hip ratio has been calculated at ages 43 (WHR89 – calculated from ABC189 and HIP189) and 53 years (WHR99 – calculated from ABC99 and HIPC99).

Some erroneous outliers were found in WHR99 where Waist-Hip ratios of 1 or lower were present. On further inspection it appears the ratio was calculated using missing values. This accounts for just 4 values.
