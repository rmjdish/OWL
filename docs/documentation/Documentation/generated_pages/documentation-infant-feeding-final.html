Infant feeding

Three additional variables have now been derived using 2 variables: Breast Feeding [BRE] and Weaning [WEA] derived in 1948 from data collected in follow-up survey in 1948. The new variables derived indicate the type (INITFEED) and length of feeding (LENGTHBT, LENGTHBR) when SM was an infant.

BRE

Purpose : Breast feeding at age 2yrs Year : 1948

1. Definition 0 Never breast fed 1 Stopped 1st month 2 Stopped 2nd month 3 Stopped 3rdmonth 4 Stopped 4th month 5 Stopped 5-7 months 6 Stopped 8-10 months 7 Stopped after 10 months -9899 Unknown -9999 Unknown (blank on rubric card)

2. Specification

Coded indirectly from 1948 follow-up survey (form=AN1) from answers to Q.16.

WEA

Purpose : Weaning at age 2yrs Year : 1948

1. Definition 0 19-24 months and not yet weaned from breast (if not bottle fed) 1 01-07 months – from bottle 2 08-10 months – from bottle 3 11-12 months – from bottle 4 13-18 months – from bottle 5 19-24 months – and not yet weaned from bottle 6 01-07 months – from breast (when not bottle fed) 7 08-10 months – from breast (when not bottle fed) 8 11-12 months - from breast (when not bottle fed) 9 13-18 months – from breast (when not bottle fed) -9899 Unknown

2. Specification

Coded indirectly from 1948 follow-up survey (form=AN1) from answers to Q.16.

INITFEED

Purpose : Type of feeding at age 2yrs Year : 1948

1. Definition 0 Bottle only 1 Breast only 2 Mixed 3 Some breast feeding, if bottle fed unknown 4 Some bottle feeding, if breast fed unknown 9 Unknown

2. Specification

Compute initfeed=99. if(bre=0) initfeed=0. if((wea=0 or (wea ge 6 and wea le 9)) and (bre gt 0 and bre le 7)) initfeed=1. if((bre ge 1 and bre le 5) and (wea ge 1 and wea le 5)) initfeed=2. if(bre=6 and (wea ge 2 and wea le 5)) initfeed=2. if(bre=7 and (wea ge 3 and wea le 5)) initfeed=2. if(any(bre,6,7) and wea=1) initfeed=2. if(bre=7 and wea=2) initfeed=2. if((bre gt 0 and bre le 7) and wea=-9899) initfeed=3. if((wea gt 0 and wea le 7) and bre lt 0) initfeed=4.

LENGTHBR

Purpose : Length of breast feeding in months at 2yrs Year : 1948

1. Definition

(mid-point values used)

4 1-7 mths (wea=6) 9 8-10 mths (wea=7) 11.5 11-12 mths (wea=8) 15.5 13-18 mths (wea=9) 21.5 19-24 mths & not yet weaned from breast ( if not bottle fed) (wea=0) 99 Unknown

2. Specification

if(wea=6) lengthbr=4. if(wea=7) lengthbr=9. if(wea=8) lengthbr=11.5. if(wea=9) lengthbr=15.5. if(wea=0) lengthbr=21.5

LENGTHBT

Purpose : Length of bottle feeding in months at 2yrs Year : 1948

1. Definition

(mid-point values used)

4 1-7 mths (wea=1) 9 8-10 mths (wea=2) 11.5 11-12 mths (wea=3) 15.5 13-18 mths (wea=4) 21.5 19-24 mths & not yet weaned from bottle (wea=5) 99 Unknown

2. Specification

if(wea=1) lengthbt=4. if(wea=2) lengthbt=9. if(wea=3) lengthbt=11.5. if(wea=4) lengthbt=15.5. if(wea=5) lengthbt=21.5.
