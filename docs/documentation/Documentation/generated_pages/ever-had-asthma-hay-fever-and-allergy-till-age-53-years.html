Asthma

The variable combines the information on whether they have ever had asthma up to 1989 with information on whether they have had asthma in the last ten years reported in 1999. Binary yes /no variable.

evasthm - Ever had asthma up to 1999?

Derivation

Step1 was to use all the responses given in 1999 to the question [have you had asthma in the last 10 years] only for those people who also had a valid response in 1989.

Step2 was to update the yes category for any who had reported no in 1999 but had reported yes in 1989.

Raw variables used: asth89 asthma
