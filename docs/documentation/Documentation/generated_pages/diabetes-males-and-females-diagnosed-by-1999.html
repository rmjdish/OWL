Classification of those males diagnosed as having diabetes by 1999 (age 53 years)

The purpose of this is to establish who can be reliably regarded as having T2 diabetes. I used the flat file of medication from 1977, Mike Wadsworth’s notes on those diagnosed as diabetic until age of 36 (1982), the variable dagec and diabec, as well as AKIDD99 (1 to 6).

The procedure involved an initial classification of anyone with diabetes diagnosed under age of 20 being assumed to have T1 and anyone over 50 assumed to have T2. Then medications were checked from microfiche and MW records and from medication file from 1977 to 1999. Any woman whose diabetes was diagnosed within a year of the birth of one of her children was assessed as to whether she had GDM.
