Divorce 1999

Variables recording age at parent divorce (div and da72) have been cleaned if missing or inconsistent. Early nurse reports (1950-1961) were used to correctly code age at divorce of parents creating new variables divscage and divsbc.

DIVSCAGE

Purpose : Age at which parents divorce (history variable) Year : 1999

1. Definition 88 No divorce 99 Unknown

DIVSBC

Purpose : Age at which parents divorce (history variable) categories Year : 1999

1. Definition 1 0 to 4 years 2 5 to 11 years 3 12 to 17 years 4 18 to 26 years 88 No divorce 99 Unknown

2. Specification

RECODE DIVSCAGE (0 THRU 4=1)(5 THRU 11=2)(12 THRU 17=3)(18 THRU 26=4) (ELSE=COPY) INTO DIVSBC
