Cognitive function in childhood, adolescence and early adulthood

Cognitive function was first tested at age 8 years in a school setting, and again in this setting at ages 11 and 15 years. A test of verbal comprehension given at the latter age was then re-administered at age 26 years (with 10 additional items of increased difficulty to avoid a ceiling effect), as part of the first interview of the survey member as an adult.

Age 8 years

Raw variables (N.B. all these are included in the ‘cogchad’ save file) VOC8R – a 50-item vocabulary test SC8R – a 35-item verbal comprehension (sentence completion) test R8R – mechanical reading (pronunciation) using the same words as for vocabulary PI8R – a 60-item non-verbal ability (picture intelligence) test For all ‘R’ variables 99 indicates missing data VOC8N – as above for vocabulary but normalised to a mean of 100 and an SD of 15 SC8N - as above for sentence completion but normalised to a mean of 100 and an SD of 15 R8N - as above for mechanical reading but normalised to a mean of 100 and an SD of 15 PI8N - as above for picture intelligence but normalised to a mean of 100 and an SD of 15 For all ‘N’ variables 999 indicates missing data

Derived summary variable COG8H – RH’s standardised cognitive score at age8 - standardised for whole population available on each test see note below.

Formula COG8H=(VOC8H+SC8H+R8H+PI8H)/4

Where VOC8H, SC8H, R8H, PI8H are the standardised to the whole population available on each test versions of the variables VOC8R, SC8R, R8R, PI8R.

Age 11 years

Raw variables (N.B. all these are included in the ‘cogchad’ save file) GA11R – an 80-item verbal and non-verbal ability test V11R – the 40 verbal items from above NV11R – the 40 non-verbal items from above VOC11R – the same 50 words used at age 8 years for vocabulary R11R - the same 50 words used at age 8 years for mechanical reading A11R – a 50-item arithmetic test For all ‘R’ variables 99 indicates missing data except GA11R, where 999 is used GA11N – as above for the verbal and non-verbal ability test but normalised to a mean of 100 and an SD of 15 V11N - as above for the verbal ability test items but normalised to a mean of 100 and an SD of 15 NV11N - as above for the non-verbal ability test items but normalised to a mean of 100 and an SD of 15 VOC11N - as above for the vocabulary items but normalised to a mean of 100 and an SD of 15 R11N - as above for the mechanical reading items but normalised to a mean of 100 and an SD of 15 A11N - as above for the arithmetic items but normalised to a mean of 100 and an SD of 15 For all ‘N’ variables 999 indicates missing data

Derived summary variable COG11H – RH’s standardised cognitive score at age11 - standardised for whole population available on each test see note below.

Formula

COG11H=(R11H+VOC11H+GA11H+A11H)/4

Where R11H, VOC11H, GA11H, A11H are the standardised to the whole population available on each test versions of the variables R11R, VOC11R, GA11R, A11R

Age 15 years

Raw variables (N.B. all these are included in the ‘cogchad’ save file) GA15R – the Heim AH4 Group Ability Test, a 130-item verbal and non-verbal ability test V15R – the 65 verbal items from above NV15R – the 65 non-verbal items from above R15R – the Watts Vernon Reading Test, a 35-item verbal comprehension (sentence completion) test M15R – a 47-item mathematics test For all ‘R’ variables 99 indicates missing data, except for GA15R, where 999 is used GA15N – as above for the Heim AH4 Group Ability Test but normalised to a mean of 100 and an SD of 15 V15N - as above for the verbal items of the AH4 but normalised to a mean of 100 and an SD of 15 NV15N - as above for the non-verbal items of the AH4 but normalised to a mean of 100 and an SD of 15 R15N - as above for the Watts Vernon but normalised to a mean of 100 and an SD of 15 M15N – as above for mathematics but normalised to a mean of 100 and an SD of 15 For all ‘N’ variables 999 indicates missing data

Derived summary variable COG15H – RH’s standardised cognitive score at age15 - standardised for whole population available on each test see note below.

Formula COG15H=(R15H+GA15H+M15H)/3

Where R15H, GA15H, M15H are the standardised to the whole population available on each test versions of the variables R15R, GA15R, M15R. 5/02/14: Updated by KM to revise statistics for variable GA15R [Final version of this data on the road map produced slightly different figures from the original documentation]
