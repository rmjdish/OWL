APOE status (Phase 1 Insight 46) (2019-CL)

APOE genotype was determined using the two SNPs, rs439358 and rs7412, using genetic data derived from a blood sample at age 53,1 or if missing, a blood sample at age 69-71 years. Individuals were coded according to the presence of e2, e3 and e4 alleles. Two Insight 46 participants did not have available data at age 53 or 69-71 years.
