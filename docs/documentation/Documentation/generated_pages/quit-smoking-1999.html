Summary

The quit smoking variables were derived in response to a request from Martin Widschwendter. His team required the time between quitting smoking and sample donation at the age of 53.

These variables are: quit, quit2

Description of the quit smoking variables

The variable ‘quit’ records the study year when SM changed from either a ‘Never’ or ‘Current’ smoker’ to an ‘Ex-smoker’. The last year this transition was made is recorded and marks the LAST (most recent) quit.

The variable ‘quit2’ records the study year when SM changed from either a ‘Never’ or ‘Current smoker’ to an ‘Ex-smoker’. The first year this transition was made is recorded and marks the FIRST (earliest) quit.
