Parental Bonding Instrument

The Parental Bonding Instrument was included on the 1989 self completion questionnaire. Of the 25 items in the scale one was omitted (item 4: Seemed emotionally cold to me), and 5 items were reworded from a negative direction to a positive direction (items 2, 8, 14, 18 and 24). The scale using NSHD was originally created by Bryan Rodgers and used in Reported parental behaviour and adult affective symptoms. 1. Associations and moderating factors and Reported parental behaviour and adult affective symptoms. 2. Mediating factors. (Psychol Med. 1996 Jan;26(1):51-61. and Psychol Med. 1996 Jan;26(1):63-77). The scale was subsequently checked by J Withey and this documentation was produced. There is no difference in the 2 sets of scales produced.
