Metabolic syndrome at age 53 years

Date: 14/05/09 (edited 25/06/09)

Documentation by: DG edited BL

Please, note, that initially all coding was done by Claudia Langenberg.

Metabolic syndrome definition

The metabolic syndrome and its components were defined using cut points recommended by ATPIII8 (2001). We modified the ATPIII8 definition to include glycated haemoglobin (HbA1c) instead of fasting plasma glucose, data for which were unavailable. Participants were classified as having the metabolic syndrome if they met any 3 of the following criteria: 1) waist circumference > 102 cm for men or >88 cm for women, 2) triglyceride level ≥ 1.7 mmol/L (150 mg/dL), Since the triglyceride level was measured using non-fasting blood, we additionally defined another threshold - in the top gender-specific quarter of the distribution (≥ 3.0 mmol/L for men and ≥ 2.2 for women). 3) HDL cholesterol level < 1.036 mmol/L for men or <1.295 mmol/L for women, 4) blood pressure level: either SBP ≥ 130 or DBP ≥ 85 mm Hg. Participants using antihypertensive medications were considered as meeting high blood pressure. 5) HbA1c level in the top gender-specific quarter of the distribution (> 5.8% among both men and women). Participants using medications for diabetes were considered as meeting HbA1c criteria. The save file also includes some raw and derived variables that can be useful for defining metabolic syndrome with other clinical criteria (WHO or IDF) if it is necessary.

Raw variables and variables derived previously

SBP992 – systolic blood pressure at age 53; DBP992 – diastolic blood pressure at age 53; Missing for all reasons is coded to 9999 for all variables. These are second measures of the blood pressure: All are in mmHg. These variables can be picked up from: $der/final/cvd/bp

HBA99N – glycated haemoglobin (%) at age 53; CHOL99N – total cholesterol (mmol/l) at age 53; TRIG99N – triglycerides (mmol/l) at age 53; HDL99N – high density lipoprotein cholesterol (mmol/l) at age 53; Note: HDL values of 8 are excluded; LDL99N – low density lipoprotein cholesterol (mmol/l) at age 53; Missing values are then 77 (no blood taken?), 98 (different missing data), 99 (no interview) The location of these variables is $der/final/cvd/bloods

ABC – abdominal (waist) circumference (cm) at age 53; ** HIPC – hips circumference (cm) at age 53, cm These original variables were recoded to ABC99C and HIPC99C** with missing: 999 (no interview, based on Q99A answer) and 988 (different missing data). These variables are kept together in the data file. The original variables can be picked up from $xlb/capi99a/c.sv The recoded variables can be picked up from $der/final/waisthip/

Q99A – whether the Survey member was interviewed in 1999: 0=no, 1=yes

BMI99U – BMI (body mass index) in 1999 in kg/(m2); can be picked up from the derived library $der\final\htwtbmi\d080422.sv

B02299A, B02499A, B02599A, B026299A – antihypertensive medication: diuretics, beta blockers, RA system drugs+others, calcium channel blockers, correspondingly; B06199A – medication for diabetes; B212099A – medication for dislipidemia. These variables can be picked up from $xlb/bnf99a/d020619.sv

DIABTY - Type of diabetes 1=Type 1 - diagnosed younger age (28 or less in this group) and on insulin since diagnosis 2=Type 2 - diagnosed at older age (30 or more in this group) and only ever on diet or OHA, or insulin added more than 2 years after diagnosis 3=GDM- diagnosed in pregnancy and no further record of treatment for diabetes 4=Unknown type 5=Unsure ? type 1 / type 2 – see notes in document: males_and_females_diagnosed_by_1999 6=Unsure ? type 2 / type 1 – see notes in document: males_and_females_diagnosed_by_1999 88 =No diabetes 99=Missing This variable can be picked up from the derived library: $is/diabetes/derived/d090305.sv

MEDBP99 – antihypertensive medication use at age 53 0 = no antihypertensive medication use 1 = antihypertensive medication use 999 = missing

WHR99 – waist-hip ratio at age 53 (can be useful for WHO metabolic syndrome definition) 999 = missing

The metabolic syndrome variables

The variables below are to be found: $der/final/cvd/bloods/metab/

Derived variables

DIABMS – diabetes status at age 53 recoded for metabolic syndrome 0 = no diabetes (including those with gestational diabetes) 1 = diabetes (except those with gestational diabetes) 999 = missing

WCBIN99 – high waist circumference at age 53 0 = no (≤102 cm for men or ≤88 cm for women) 1 = yes (>102 cm for men or >88 cm for women) 999 = missing

SHYP992M – high systolic blood pressure or antihypertensive medication use at age 53 0 = no (<130 mmHg and no antihypertensive medication use) 1 = yes (≥130 mmHg or antihypertensive medication use) 999 = missing

DHYP992M – high diastolic blood pressure or antihypertensive medication use at age 53 0 = no (<85 mmHg and no antihypertensive medication use) 1 = yes (≥85 mmHg or antihypertensive medication use) 999 = missing

HYP992M – hypertension or antihypertensive medication use at age 53 0 = no (<130/85 mmHg and no antihypertensive medication use) 1 = yes (≥130/85 mmHg or antihypertensive medication use) 999 = missing

HBABIN99 – high level of glycated haemoglobin (using quartile as cut-off) at age 53y
0 = no (≤ 5.8%) 1 = yes (> 5.8% ) 999 = missing

HBABIN99M - high level of glycated haemoglobin (using quartile as cut-off) or medication for diabetes at age 53y. 0 = no (≤5.8% and no medication for diabetes) 1 = yes (> 5.8% or medication for diabetes) 999 = missing Note: There are 5 cases with type 1 diabetes with missing information on medication for diabetes. The script can be adjusted for this if necessary.

HDLBIN99 – low level of high density lipoprotein at age 53y 0 = no (≥ 036 mmol/L for men or <1.295 mmol/L for women) 1 = yes (< 1.036 mmol/L for men or <1.295 mmol/L for women) 999 = missing

TRIGBIN99 – high level of triglyceride at age 53y using ATPIII criteria 0 = no (< 1.7 mmol/L) 1 = yes (≥ 1.7 mmol/L) 999 = missing

TRIGBIN99X – high level of triglyceride at age 53y using quartile as a cut-off 0 = no (< 3.0 mmol/L for men or < 2.2 mmol/L for women) 1 = yes (≥ 3.0 mmol/L for men or ≥ 2.2 mmol/L for women) 999 = missing

Note: We did not use the medications for cholesterol while deriving variables HDLBIN and TRIGBIN since these medications do not affect HDL and triglyceride level.

MSSCORE99 – number of metabolic syndrome symptoms at age 53y (with TRIGBIN) This is the sum of the values of the categorical variables WCBIN99 HYP992M HBACLBIN HDLBIN TRIGBIN; only those with complete information on all of these variables are included 1 – 5 = valid score 999 = missing

MSSCORE99X – number of metabolic syndrome symptoms at age 53y (with TRIGBINX) This is the sum of the values of the categorical variables WCBIN99 HYP992M HBACLBIN HDLBIN TRIGBINX; only those with complete information on all of these variables are included 1 – 5 = valid score 999 = missing

METABOL99 – metabolic syndrome at age 53y (if 3 or more symptoms: WCBIN99, HYP992M, HBACLBIN, HDLBIN, TRIGBIN, presented) 0 = no (<3 symptoms presented) 1 = yes (≥3 symptoms presented) 999 = missing

METABOL99X – metabolic syndrome at age 53y (if 3 or more symptoms: WCBIN99, HYP992M, HBACLBIN, HDLBIN, TRIGBINX, presented) 0 = no (<3 symptoms presented) 1 = yes (≥3 symptoms presented) 999 = missing
