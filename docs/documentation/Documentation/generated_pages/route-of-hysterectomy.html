Route of hysterectomy

Women’s hysterectomy status has been ascertained from age 43 years onwards (see derived hysterectomy status variables and accompanying documentation). Information on route of hysterectomy (i.e. abdominal, vaginal, keyhole surgery) was collected in the 1997, 1998, 1999 and 2000 ‘Women’s Health in the Middle Years’ questionnaires and also in the 2005 Hysterectomy study questionnaire. The information provided in each of these questionnaires was combined (with preference given to the earliest information provided) to create the variable ROUTEC.

ROUTEC: Route of hysterectomy 1 = Abdominal 2 = Vaginal 3 = Keyhole surgery 99 = Had hysterectomy but route unknown 888 = Not applicable (i.e. did not have hysterectomy, hysterectomy status unknown or male)
