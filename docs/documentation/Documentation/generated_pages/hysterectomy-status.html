Hysterectomy status up to age 57 years

During the 1989 home visit, when participants were aged 43 years, women were asked by research nurses whether their periods had stopped, the age they stopped and whether the reason for this was natural menopause, hysterectomy or hysterectomy plus oophorectomy.

In each of the ‘Women’s Health in the Middle Years’ postal questionnaires sent annually between 1993 and 2000 (age 47 to 54y), inclusive, and in 2003 (age 57y), women were asked whether they had undergone any one of the following five procedures: hysterectomy and bilateral oophorectomy; hysterectomy only; hysterectomy and unilateral oophorectomy; bilateral oophorectomy; unilateral oophorectomy. During the 1999 home visit women who had not responded regularly to the ‘Women’s Health in the Middle Years’ questionnaires were again asked, in a self-completion questionnaire, whether they had ever undergone any one of the five procedures

The variables are:
WOP1C, WOP2C, WOP3C, WOP4C, WOP5C WOPALLC, HYSALL3C DWOP1CC, DWOP2CC, DWOP3CC, DWOP4CC, DWOP5CC DWOP1XX, DWOP2XX, DWOP3XX, DWOP4XX, DWOP5XX DWOPDC, DWOPAGE

WOP (women’s operation) variables

Using information from each of the annual women’s health postal questionnaires and the questionnaire completed during the home visit in 1999, 5 variables, one for each of the different operations, were generated. Where there were differences in the information provided by women at different data collection points an order of preference was established to ensure these were dealt with in a standard way. For example, where women reported more than one of the five operations and this was compatible e.g. a hysterectomy at one time and a bilateral oophorectomy at another time, the women were coded as having had the combined operation e.g. hysterectomy and bilateral oophorectomy and allocated the date of procedure of the hysterectomy. By coding women’s responses in this way, no woman can be coded as having more than 1 of the 5 operations.

Important note: This first set of 5 variables (wop1c to wop5c) do not incorporate information from the 1989 home visit because of the less detailed way in which information on hysterectomy was ascertained at this time. For this reason WOPALLC, which does include information from 1989 (see details below), should be used in analyses.

WOP1C = Hysterectomy and bilateral oophorectomy reported in a postal questionnaire between 1993 and 2003 or at home visit in 1999 0 = Did not have operation 1 = Had operation 9 = Missing (returned at least one questionnaire but did not answer appropriate questions) 88 = Unknown (did not return any of the appropriate questionnaires) 888 = Male

WOP2C = Hysterectomy reported in a postal questionnaire between 1993 and 2003 or at home visit in 1999 0 = Did not have operation 1 = Had operation 9 = Missing (returned at least one questionnaire but did not answer appropriate questions) 88 = Unknown (did not return any of the appropriate questionnaires) 888 = Male

WOP3C = Hysterectomy and unilateral oophorectomy reported in a postal questionnaire between 1993 and 2003 or at home visit in 1999 0 = Did not have operation 1 = Had operation 9 = Missing (returned at least one questionnaire but did not answer appropriate questions) 88 = Unknown (did not return any of the appropriate questionnaires) 888 = Male

WOP4C = Bilateral oophorectomy reported in a postal questionnaire between 1993 and 2003 or at home visit in 1999 0 = Did not have operation 1 = Had operation 9 = Missing (returned at least one questionnaire but did not answer appropriate questions) 88 = Unknown (did not return any of the appropriate questionnaires) 888 = Male

WOP5C = Unilateral oophorectomy reported in a postal questionnaire between 1993 and 2003 or at home visit in 1999 0 = Did not have operation 1 = Had operation 9 = Missing (returned at least one questionnaire but did not answer appropriate questions) 88 = Unknown (did not return any of the appropriate questionnaires) 888 = Male

These 5 variables have been combined along with information on women’s operations reported in 1989 to generate a variable which summarises women’s reports of hysterectomy and oophorectomy in any questionnaire between 1989 and 2003 inclusive (i.e. up to age 57y). As this combined variable also includes information from 1989 it should be used in preference to the variables WOP1C to WOP5C.

WOPALLC: Hysterectomy and/or oophorectomy reported between 1989 and 2003 (incl) 0 = No hysterectomy or oophorectomy 1 = Hysterectomy and bilateral oophorectomy 2 = Hysterectomy only 3 = Hysterectomy and unilateral oophorectomy 4 = Bilateral oophorectomy 5 = Unilateral oophorectomy 9 = Missing (returned at least one questionnaire but did not answer appropriate questions) 88 = Not known (did not return any of the relevant questionnaires) 888 = Male

Recode of WOPALLC: HYSALL3C – Report of any hysterectomy vs none (up to 2003) 0 = No hysterectomy (including those who just had an oophorectomy) 1 = Hysterectomy with or without oophorectomy 9 = Missing (returned at least one questionnaire but no info on operations provided)

Dates of operations

As well as reporting whether or not they had undergone a hysterectomy and/or oophorectomy women were also asked to report the month and year or their age in years at the time of operation. From this information dates of operations were calculated. Where different dates were reported at different data collection points the first reported date of the operation was taken as the correct date. Wherever possible (i.e. if month and year of operation had been reported) age in months since birth was calculated, or if this could not be done, age in years was used, or if this had also not been reported, year of operation was taken.

‘Best available’ date variables for each of the 5 operation types: DWOP1CC: Date of hysterectomy and bilateral oophorectomy DWOP2CC: Date of hysterectomy only DWOP3CC: Date of hysterectomy and unilateral oophorectomy DWOP4CC: Date of bilateral oophorectomy DWOP5CC: Date of unilateral oophorectomy All 5 variables follow the same coding format: 9 = reported this operation but date missing 22 to 52 = age in years 63 to 94 = 1963 to 1994 (year of operation) 212 to 682 = months since birth 888 = did not have this operation (or unknown hysterectomy status) 997 = returned at least one questionnaire but did not report any info on operations 8888 = male For each of these 5 date variables, the number of women with a date value <888 is equal to the number of women who reported undergoing this operation (as coded in wopallc).

DWOP1CC to DWOP5CC have been recoded so that all dates are also available in age in years: DWOP1XX to DWOP5XX = age in years of corresponding operation for all those who made a report between 1989 and 2003 (equivalent to DWOP1CC to DWOP5CC but with all dates as ages in years) All 5 variables follow the same coding format: 17 to 57 = age in years 888 = did not have this operation (or unknown hysterectomy status) 997 = returned at least one questionnaire but did not report any info on operations 999 = had this operation but date missing 8888 = male

Information on timings of each of the 5 individual operations has been combined into 2 variables: DWOPDC = combined date variable – most accurate date of operation, whichever of the five operations this was 22 to 52 = age in years 63 to 94 = 1963 to 1994 212 to 682 = months since birth 888 = did not report having one of the five operations or did not return any of the relevant questionnaires 997 = returned a questionnaire but did not report any info on ops 999 = reported one of the 5 operations but date missing 8888 = male

DWOPAGE = combined date variable – age (in years) at operation, whichever of the five operations this was 17 to 56 = age in years 888 = did not report having one of the five operations or did not return any of the relevant questionnaires 997 = returned a questionnaire but did not report any info on ops 999 = reported one of the 5 operations but date missing 8888 = male
