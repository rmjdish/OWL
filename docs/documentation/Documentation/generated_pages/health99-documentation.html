Angina (based on Rose angina questionnaire) (ANG99Q)

Angina is defined by ever having pain/discomfort in your chest (ANGIN=1) + the pain when you walk uphill or hurry, or pain when you walk at an ordinary pace (CHPRU=1 or 3, or, CHPRN=1 or 3) + the sm stopping or slowing down when they get pain on walking (CHPRR = 1 or 3) + the pain being relieved on standing still (CHPRS=1) + the pain goes away in 10 minutes or less (CHPRT =1) + the pain is located in the sternum or left chest/ arm (CHRRL 1 thru 5 = 1 thru 4).

Overall angina variable (ANG99C)

‘No angina’ is defined by a negative response to Q25a (ANGE). ‘Past angina’ is defined by sm not having taken angina medication in the last 12 months (PANG) or no Dr consultations in the last 12 months (CCPRGH31). ‘Current angina’ is defined as sms who have taken medication for their angina in the past 12 months (PANG) or having consulted a Dr in the past 12 months (CCPRGH). ‘Angina according to LSH Qs’ - see above.

Intermittent claudication according to the Rose intermittent claudication questionnaire (CL99)

Intermittent claudication is defined by getting pain in either leg on walking (LGP =1 or 3), + the leg pain not beginning when standing still or sitting (LGPBS =2), + the pain being felt in the calf (LGPPT=1), + the pain being present when walking uphill or in a hurry (LGPU=1 or 3), or pain being felt when walking at an ordinary pace on the level (LGPN=1 or 3), + the pain doesn’t disappear whilst walking (LGPD=2), + sm stops or slows down when pain felt whilst walking (LGPR=1), + when standing still the pain is relieved (LGPX=2), + the pain goes away in less than 10 minutes (LGPT=1).

Heart attack (HATT99)

Having suffered a diagnosed heart attack is defined by a positive response to Q28c (HARAD1=1 or 2). Having suffered an undiagnosed heart attack is defined by a negative response to Q28c (HARAD1=3). ‘Never’ is defined by a negative response to Q28a (HARAE=2).

0 Never 2940 1 Diagnosed heart attack 46 2 Undiagnosed heart attack <10 8 No interview 2374

Stroke (STROKE99)

‘Diagnosed stroke’ is defined by a positive response to Q31b (SDOCTOLD=1), whilst ‘undiagnosed stroke’ is defined by SDOCTOLD=2. ‘Never’ suffered a stroke is defined by a negative response to Q31a (STROKEC=2).

0 Never 2963 1 Diagnosed stroke 23 2 Undiagnosed stroke <10 8 No interview 2374

Type of heart trouble

Q29 asks sm's to give a more specific description of their heart problems: Looking at this card, please tell me which of these kinds of heart trouble you have had, if any, in the last ten years that is since you were 43 years old? Coronary thrombosis (n=5) Myocardial infarction (n=28) Valvular disease (n=<10) Aortic stenosis (n=<10) Ischaemic heart disease (n=12) Tachycardia (n=31) Palpitations or heart murmer (n=302) Other (n=59) None (n=2544) It was decided that those who experienced Valvular disease (HEART1=3) or Aortic stenosis (HEART1=4) should be included in the overall CVD variable (CVD99=1).

Respiratory variable as used in Gerontology paper (RESPN99)

‘No resp problems’ is defined as sm’s who were interviewed in 1999 but did not respond to the respiratory questions (Q99A=1). ‘Respiratory problems' included those who coughed more than 3 months a year (WIM =1) or brought up phlegm on most days more than 3 months a year (PHLM=1) or were wheezy most days or nights or suffered more than 1 chest illness in the last 3 years (NUM=2). ** 0 **No resp problems 2417 1 Resp problems 568 8 No interview 2374 9 Unknown <10

Diabetes at 53 yrs = ENDO99 (Overall endocrine variable)

‘Diagnosed diabetes’ is defined by a positive response to the question Have you been told by a doctor you have diabetes in the last 10 years? (DIABC=1). ‘No diabetes’ is the negative response to this question (DIABC=2).

Cancer at 53 yrs = CANC99 (Overall cancer variable)

‘Diagnosed cancer’ is defined by a positive response to the question Have you been told by a doctor you have cancer in the last 10 years? (CDOCTOLD=1), whilst ‘undiagnosed cancer’ is defined by the negative response (CDOCTOLD=2, unknown is cancer=1, whilst cdoctold=2).

Epilepsy at 53 yrs (EPI99)

‘Diagnosed epilepsy’ is a positive response to the question Has a doctor ever told you have epilepsy? (FDOCTOLD=1, undiagnose=2). ‘No epilepsy’ is defined by an sm never experiencing a fit (FIT=2).

0 No epilepsy 2941 1 Diagnosed epilepsy 43 2 Undiagnosed epilepsy <10 8 No interview 2374

ME at 53 yrs (ME99)

‘No ME’ is defined by a negative response to Have you ever had ME? (MEC=2) ‘Diagnosed ME’ is defined by a positive response to the question Has a doctor ever told you have ME? (MEDOCTOLD=1, undiagnosed ME=2).

Health variables at 43 years

As indicated above, many of the health variables available at 53 years cannot be exactly replicated using the 43-year data. In 1999 survey members were typically asked if a do ctor had told them they had a specified health problem and this was taken as a positive response, however in 1989 members were asked whether they have had the specified health problem, and then whether they have seen a health professional about it in the last year. So a similar variable could be created if desired.
