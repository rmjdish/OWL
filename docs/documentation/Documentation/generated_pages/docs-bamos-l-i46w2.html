Longitudinal BaMoS outcome variables Phase1 and Phase 2 Insight 46

This document details all variables created from the longitudinal Bayesian Model Selection (BaMoS) pipeline (Insight46 phase 1 and phase 2), how they were cleaned and quality controlled (QC), and recommendations for use.

Global BaMoS outcome variables

Phase 1 and Phase 2 longitudinal WMH masks were generated from mid-point registered FLAIR images by Carole Sudre using BaMoS (Sudre et al., 2015). Global WMH volumes were generated from all available masks at both time-points, and are expressed in millilitres. Longitudinal change score was generated by simply subtracting longitudinal Phase 1 WMH volume from longitudinal Phase 2 WMH volume.

Longitudinal WMH mask QC and inclusion criteria

QC of all WMH masks was performed by Matt Storey, Jo Barnes, Carole Sudre and Sarah-Naomi James by visually inspecting masks overlaid on corresponding FLAIR images. Masks with segmentation issues were re-run with modified scripts and corrected. All corrected masks were reviewed by Jo Barnes and Tom Brown in order to determine if the corrections had solved the segmentation issues and were appropriate for use. There were no masks that failed QC other than on the basis of stroke lesions affecting segmentation. Individuals who had evidence of cortical stroke or clinically reported autoimmune demyelination were excluded from the dataset (Table 1). Individuals with cortical stroke identified from any available source (including radiology reads, BaMoS QC and clinical reports) at either time-point were visually inspected by Jo Barnes and Tom Brown and excluded. The purpose of this was to prevent the inclusion or misclassification of other hyperintense imaging characteristics (stroke and MS lesions) that resemble WMH of presumed vascular origin (age-related cerebral small vessel disease). Individuals with other neurological disorders were not otherwise excluded at this stage (see “Recommended Complimentary Datasets”).

Recommendations for Use

This data is intended for longitudinal analysis, and should be used to reflect WMH volume change between Phase 1 and Phase 2 (bamos_wmhv_change_long). For cross-sectional analysis of Phase 1 global WMH volume, we recommend using “lesiontot”. For cross-sectional analysis using only Phase 2 WMH volumes, we recommend using cross-sectional Phase 2 WMHV which is yet to be shared at date of longitudinal dataset publication. This is because WMH volumes calculated in a longitudinal framework from mid-point registered images tend to be a more conservative estimate of WMH volume, incorporating information from both time-points. If only interested in WMH volume at a single time-point, cross-sectional measurements are more appropriate. For longitudinal analysis of WMH volume change, it is recommended that only this dataset is used, rather than a combination of cross-sectional measurements of WMH volume from Phase 1 and Phase 2.

Recommended Complimentary Datasets

Insight46 MRI QC Summary Phase 1 and 2 Use to determine T1, T2 and FLAIR QC pass/fail for use in combination with other imaging data.

Clinical Diagnosis of Neurological Disorders Phase 1 and Phase 2 In this longitudinal WMH volume dataset we have excluded individuals with any evidence of cortical stroke as well as individuals with clinically reported autoimmune demyelination (Table 1). Depending on intended analysis, researchers may also wish to exclude other neurological conditions that have been shown to influence WMH volume changes.

Total Intracranial Volume It is recommended that analyses using WMH data are adjusted for TIV.

Variables:

p1_bamoswmhvl_i46p2 – global WMH volume at baseline (Phase 1) in millilitres calculated using longitudinal BaMoS. p2_bamoswmhvl_i46p2 – global WMH volume at follow-up (Phase 2) in millilitres calculated using longitudinal BaMoS. bamos_wmhvc_i46p2 – change in WMH volume from baseline to follow-up (p2_bamoswmhvl_i46p2 – p1_bamoswmhvl_i46p2).
