Smoking pack years data up to 60-64 years

For Submission to the NSHD Scientific Support Team | Description of dataset | Information on smoking tobacco was obtained across the entire life course in the NSHD at ages 20, 25, 31, 36, 43, 53 and 60+ years. From age 36 onwards, information was obtained during a home-interview conducted by research nurses, while data at earlier years were collected by postal questionnaires. Cohort members who provided an affirmative response to being a current cigarette smoker were classified as ‘smokers’, regardless of the quantity of cigarettes smoked per day, while those who provided a negative response were classified as ‘non-smokers’. | | Summary of work undertaken | Cleaning Description/Rationale: Cleaning and deriving the smoking pack years variable. The number of pack years per person represents the average number of cigarettes smoked per day divided by 20 (the number of cigarettes in a standard pack), multiplied by the number of years an individual has been smoking. For example, one cigarette pack-year represents smoking one pack of cigarettes a day for one year (or half a pack a day for two years). | | Names of source variables | * cig66 cig71c cig77c cig82c cig89c cig99c cig09c * smods66 smod71 smods77 smods82 smods89c smods99c smod09 * cigstart stop66 stop71 stop82 stop89 | | Output variables (please list names of new variables created) | packyr20 packyr2025 packyr2531 packyr3136 packyr3643 packyr4353 packyr20imp packyr2025imp packyr2531imp packyr3136imp packyr3643imp packyr4353imp packyrs53 packyrsimp53 packyrsimp36 packyrsimp43 packyr5363 packyr5363imp | | Name of person responsible for cleaning/derivation: | Heine – Original syntax to create variables till 53 years Dorina – Helped create the variable for 60-64 years and created variables for packyears at 36 and 43 years Valerie – Helped create the variable for 60-64 years |
