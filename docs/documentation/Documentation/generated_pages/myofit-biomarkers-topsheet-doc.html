Lab methods:

Two cardiovascular (CV) measures – Troponin-I and NT-proBNP were analysed at the BHF Cardiovascular Research Centre, University of Bristol. Results for 332 samples were returned. QC samples from the manufacturer were run during the assay, but no QC checks for individual samples, as these tests are not as sensitive to moderate haemolysis.
