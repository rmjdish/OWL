Notes Identification of conduct and emotional problems in NSHD was based on questions asked of teachers when study members were aged 13 and 15 years, using a forerunner of the Rutter A scale. Items for conduct problems refer to unpunctuality, restlessness, truancy, daydreaming, indiscipline, disobedience and lying. Items for emotional problems refer to anxiety, timidity, fearfulness, diffidence and avoidance of attention. Summary measures of these problems were created by deriving global measures for each from factor analysis (using the statistical package Mplus, which allows for item level missing data), then dividing scores for these into absent, mild and severe based on established percentile cuts. For conduct problems these were 0-75%, 75-93% and 94% or higher, respectively; and for emotional problems these were 0-50%, 50-87% and 88% or higher, respectively. For both sets of problems the percentile cut for ?severe? was guided by epidemiological evidence to capture potentially clinically diagnosable disorders.
