Notes CLOSER work package 9: Harmonised childhood environment and adult wellbeing measures. When the study members were aged 15, their mothers rated their own mental health using the Maudsley Personality Inventory (Jensen, 1958). The scale runs from 0 (low neuroticism) to 6 (high neuroticism).
