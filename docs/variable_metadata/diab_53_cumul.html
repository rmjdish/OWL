Category Description
Endocrine and metabolic conditions [51015] This category contains data from the questionnaires on whether the participant has any endocrine and metabolic conditions which includes the type of diabetes, how it is being controlled and the date of diagnosis. At age 60-64 years GP validation was also carried out.
