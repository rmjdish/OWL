Category Memberships

Category Description
National audit reading test (NART) [10126] The National Adult Reading Test (NART; Nelson & Willison, 1991). This is a pronunciation test involving 50 irregular words of increasing difficulty, chosen to violate conventional grapheme-phoneme correspondence rules. To pronounce any of the words correctly the respondent must therefore be able to recognise them in their written form rather than rely on intelligent guesswork. Thus it is effectively a test of knowledge acquisition, although it correlates with full-scale IQ.
