NotesAlcohol consumption was assessed using a food diary in which participants were asked to record information on the amount of alcohol consumed over 5 consecutive days. These intakes were summarised and coded in grams per day, using a specially designed data-entry program and analysed using in-house nutrient analysis program at MRC Human Nutrition Research.
