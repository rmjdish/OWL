CategoryDescription
Endocrine and metabolic conditions [51015]This category contains data from the questionnaires on whether the participant has any endocrine and metabolic conditions asked in 1982 (age 36 years), 1989 (age 43 years), 1999 (age 53 years), 2006-10 (age 60-64 years), 2014 (age 68 years), 2015 (age 69 years) and 2025 (age 79 years). Data collected includes the type of diabetes, how it is being controlled and the date of diagnosis. At age 60-64 years GP validation was also carried out. The CLS harmonisation dataset which also includes the covid questionnaires 2020-2022 is also included.
