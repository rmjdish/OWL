Of the 25 items in the scale one was omitted (item 4: Seemed emotionally cold to me), and 5 items were reworded from a negative direction to a positive direction (items 2, 8, 14, 18 and 24). The scale using NSHD was originally created by Bryan Rodgers and used in Reported parental behaviour and adult affective symptoms.
