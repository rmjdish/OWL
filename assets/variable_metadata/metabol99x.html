NotesThe metabolic syndrome and its components were defined using cut points recommended by ATPIII8 (2001). We modified the ATPIII8 definition to include glycated haemoglobin (HbA1c) instead of fasting plasma glucose, data for which were unavailable. Participants were classified as having the metabolic syndrome if they met any 3 of the following criteria: 1) Waist circumference > 102 cm for men or > 88 cm for women, 2) Triglyceride level ? 3.0 mmol/L for men and ? 2.2 for women 3) HDL cholesterol level < 1.036 mmol/L for men or <1.295 mmol/L for women, 4) Blood pressure level: either SBP ? 130 or DBP ? 85 mm Hg. Participants using antihypertensive medications were considered as meeting high blood pressure. 5) HbA1c level in the top gender-specific quarter of the distribution (> 5.8% among both men and women). Participants using medications for diabetes were considered as meeting HbA1c criteria.
