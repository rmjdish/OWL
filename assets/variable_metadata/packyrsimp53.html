Smoking [5251]This category contains data from the questionnaires on smoking cigarettes, pipes and vapes. Both cross-sectional and longitudinal variables are available. Participants were asked questions like, how many cigarettes they smoked and how long ago they gave up smoking and when they started. Derived smoking pack-years variables are also available.
