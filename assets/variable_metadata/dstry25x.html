Heart and circulatory conditions [51014]This category contains data from the questionnaires on whether the participant had any heart or circulatory conditions i.e. heart attacks, heart failure, pulmonary embolism, strokes and varicose veins or blood pressure problems. Discomfort in the leg and chest were also recorded. Rose angina variables were derived. Questions were asked in 1982 (age 36 years), 1989 (age 43 years), 1999 (age 53 years), 2006-10 (age 60-64 years), 2014 (age 68 years), 2015 (age 69 years), 2022 (age 76 years) and 2025 (age 79 years).
