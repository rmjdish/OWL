Heart and circulatory conditions [51014]This category contains data from the questionnaires on whether the participant had any heart or circulatory conditions i.e. heart attacks, heart failure, pulmonary embolism, strokes and varicose veins or blood pressure problems. Discomfort in the leg and chest were also recorded. Rose angina variables were derived.
