Blood pressure [1103]This category contains information on blood pressure measurements systolic/diastolic and pulse rate using the Omron digital blood pressure monitor. Mean arterial pressure and derived hypertension variables are also available. Information was also collected on the willingness of participant to have blood pressure measured. Some or all of these measures are available in 1982 (age 36 years), 1989 (age 43 years), 1999 (age 53 years), 2006-10 (age 60-64 years), 2015 (age 69 years) and 2023 (age 77 years).
