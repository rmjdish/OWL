NotesThe Townsend index is a measure of material deprivation within a population. The index uses four variables derived from census data: • Percentage unemployment (of those aged 16 and over who are economically active) • Percentage overcrowded households (of all households) • Percentage no car/vans ownership (of all households) The Townsend index is created by summing z-scores of logged unemployment, logged overcrowding, no car, and renters. • Percentage non-home owners (of all households)
