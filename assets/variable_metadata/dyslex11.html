NotesDyslexia status at age 11 has been derived using scores from reading and verbal/non-verbal ability tests undertaken in 1957 (at 11 years) The threshold for R11R was defined as 1.5 SD below mean. SD of R11R =10.51 and Mean=36.43. So threshold=36.43-(10.51*1.5) =20.66 Children were classified as dyslexic if they achieved a GAIIN score of 70 or higher and a R11R score lower than 20.66. The threshold of 70 for GA11N represents the cut-off for mild learning disability. Anything above this is considered within the normal range for general cognitive ability/IQ.
