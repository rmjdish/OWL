vasc_meddiuretic_i46p1 — Presence of Diuretics. These drugs may be prescribed to promote renal function and offload fluid (for example in heart failure) or to lower blood pressure. The group includes thiazide like diuretics (BNF221), loop diuretics (BNF222), potassium sparing diuretics (BNF223, BNF224) and other diuretics. Includes BNF groups 2.2.1, 2.2.2, 2.2.3, 2.2.4, 2.2.5, 2.2.6, 2.2.7 & 2.2.8. - Insight46 phase 1
